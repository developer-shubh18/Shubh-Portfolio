The thinking behind the work

Why engineer + designer.

Most engineers hand off a spec to a designer. Most designers hand off a mockup to an engineer. I've spent my time collapsing that gap — designing in Figma in the morning and writing the React component in the afternoon.

When I built the bilingual chat app, I didn't just architect the WebSocket layer — I designed the sentiment-aware message bubble system so the UI itself communicated emotional tone. When I built the Automata simulator, I started from the student's confusion, not the computer science textbook.

That's the mindset Demandbase is looking for: someone who asks "does this feel right to the user?" before committing a component to production.
